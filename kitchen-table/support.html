Kitchen Table

How can we help?

Kitchen Table is an iPhone cooking app by Pol Miro Omella. Follow recipes at your own pace, keep steps and timers together, and cook with people nearby.

Email polmiro+kitchentable@gmail.com for help, purchase questions, or feedback. Please include your app version, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Avoid including private recipe content unless it is needed.

Start cooking

Open a cookbook from the shelf, choose a recipe, and start cooking. Tap a step to read its details and mark completed work as you go. You can resume an unfinished cooking session from the Cooking tab.

Themes and purchases

Core cooking and the Original theme are free. Each additional theme is a separate one-time purchase. There is no subscription. To recover a purchased theme, use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple account that made the purchase. For an Apple refund request, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Rewarded theme previews

Where an ad is available, you can choose to watch it to try one theme for the duration shown in the app. Access starts after the reward is verified. Ad availability can vary; a network error or an incomplete ad does not grant access. A permanent theme purchase remains available during a temporary preview.

Where required, privacy choices appear before an ad loads. You can continue using free cooking if you decline. Available consent controls appear under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Cook with nearby devices

Nearby cooking shares recipe steps and timers with devices around you. Allow Local Network access when requested, keep the host app open, and have the host approve joining devices. If discovery fails, check Wi-Fi and Local Network permission in iOS Settings and try again. Nearby cooking does not connect people in different homes over the internet.

Privacy and terms

Read the Kitchen Table privacy policy for information about recipes, iCloud, purchases, optional ads, and your choices. The app uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement.
