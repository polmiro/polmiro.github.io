Kitchen Table

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Kitchen Table is an iOS cooking app provided by Pol Miro Omella ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, buy a theme, choose to watch a rewarded ad, or contact us.

Privacy contact: polmiro+castellers@gmail.com. This is the same developer contact used for Castellers; this policy applies specifically to Kitchen Table.

Recipes, settings and cooking progress

The app stores your recipe library, cookbook organization, settings and cooking progress on your device. System features such as widgets, Siri actions, timers and Live Activities use the app data needed to provide those features.

When iCloud is available, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to synchronize recipe-library data through your private iCloud database. This can include saved recipes, their translations and cookbook organization. Apple processes this information under its iCloud services and privacy terms. Your device and iCloud backup settings also affect how app data is stored and backed up.

If you choose to share a recipe, the app uploads a snapshot of that recipe and any included translations using CloudKit sharing. Anyone with the sharing link may be able to read that snapshot. Recipients can save their own copy; deleting your local copy does not delete copies already saved by others.

Theme purchases and access

We use Apple's App Store and RevenueCat to manage theme purchases, restore purchases and determine which themes are available to you. Development and test builds may use simulated purchases.

RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier, purchase and transaction records, product identifiers, entitlement status and expiration dates, and technical information such as app version, platform and network request information. The identifier is pseudonymous: it supports recognizing the same installation or customer without requiring a Kitchen Table account.

We also send feature interactions, paywall events and theme preferences to RevenueCat, including the current or requested theme and whether it is permanently owned. These support showing relevant theme offers, operating the purchase flow and understanding app and monetization performance. Kitchen Table does not receive your full payment card details from Apple.

Optional rewarded ads

You can choose to watch an ad to try a theme temporarily. Google AdMob provides the ads. Its SDK may process device or app identifiers permitted by iOS, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information supports serving and measuring ads, security, fraud prevention and advertising analytics. Ad personalization and permitted storage or identifiers depend on your privacy choices and applicable permissions.

RevenueCat receives ad events such as the offer being shown or accepted, ad loading, display, clicks, errors and impression revenue. When you complete a rewarded ad, Google and RevenueCat exchange reward verification information associated with your app user identifier and the ad unit. RevenueCat verifies the reward and returns the theme entitlement and its configured expiration. We do not send your recipe content or cooking instructions to AdMob or RevenueCat as part of this integration.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before an ad is requested. You can decline consent and use the app's free cooking features. Declining may affect ad personalization or ad availability. Choosing to watch an ad and consenting to advertising data use are separate choices. You can revisit available consent controls in Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Support and other disclosures

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is needed for your request.

We may disclose information when necessary to comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or prevent fraud and abuse.

Service providers

The services described above have their own privacy information:

  • Apple privacy policy — App Store, iCloud, CloudKit and system services.
  • RevenueCat privacy policy — purchases, theme access, paywall events and ad measurement or reward verification.
  • Google privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services — AdMob and consent messaging.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure hosting, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. This page does not embed advertising or analytics scripts.

Purposes, legal grounds and international processing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the requested app features and purchases, consent for advertising-related processing where required, legitimate interests in reliable service, support and fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent through the available privacy controls; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Our service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their privacy notices explain the locations, contractual arrangements and safeguards applicable to their services.

Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes above, including maintaining purchase access, resolving support requests, preventing fraud and meeting legal requirements. Different records have different retention needs; a temporary theme grant expiring does not itself erase the associated transaction or verification records.

You can remove content through the app's available controls and manage iCloud data through your Apple account settings. Removing the app may leave iCloud copies, backups, shared snapshots and provider-held purchase or ad records. Contact us if you need help locating or deleting information associated with your use of Kitchen Table.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data, object to or restrict processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Email the privacy contact above to make a request. We may need limited information to locate the relevant records and verify your request. Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. For questions about this policy or your information, contact Pol Miro Omella at polmiro+castellers@gmail.com.
